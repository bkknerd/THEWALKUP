Casino Royale

Vesper

Bond’s gin-and-vodka drink originated in Ian Fleming’s novel before its screen appearance. Modern versions often use Lillet Blanc in place of the original Kina Lillet.

Common ingredientsGin · Vodka · Lillet Blanc · Lemon twist

The Thin Man

Dry Martini
